    Make it yours

    Everything lives in Settings, and changes show up right away.

    • Motion: the gentle lean of the cards, moving weather, and how it opens and closes: fade, zoom, shatter, slide or swirl.
    • Graphics: how much weather detail the sky shows, and light, dark or adaptive cards.
    • Controls: which gestures open and close it, your keyboard shortcut and the scroll direction.
    • Calendars: which Google calendars and task lists show up, on which list, and in what colors.

    Easy on your battery

    While Waverly is closed, it just glances at the camera a few times a second. Only when something moves does a small hand detector built into your Mac take a look. The full hand tracker starts only when it sees your hand, and it rests again soon after. It also takes a break while you type, and on battery the camera pauses while your display sleeps.

    It all stays on your Mac

    The camera is read on your Mac and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Your calendar is read-only, and a task changes only when you check it off. There are no accounts and no tracking.

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Something else on your mind? Ask on GitHub.

    macOS won’t open Waverly the first time. Is that okay?

    Yes, that’s expected. Waverly isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Waverly and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

    Does video from my camera leave my Mac?

    No. Waverly looks at the camera right on your Mac. The video is never saved and never sent anywhere. There are no Waverly servers to send it to.

    Is the camera always on?

    While Waverly watches for your hand, yes, at a slow pace, so the camera light stays on. You can choose Pause camera from the menu bar at any time. It also pauses when your screen is locked or your Mac sleeps.

    What does it read from Google?

    Only your calendar events and tasks, just to show them to you. The one thing it can change is marking a task done when you check it off. It never deletes, edits or creates anything. Connecting Google is optional.

    Until Google finishes reviewing the app, you’ll see a “Google hasn’t verified this app” screen. Click Advanced, then Go to Waverly (unsafe).

    Do I need to install anything else?

    No. The first time it opens, Waverly downloads its hand tracker on its own. That’s about 250 MB, just once, and you can keep working while it does.

    How do I uninstall it?

    If you connected Google, first go to Settings › Google and click Disconnect, so Google drops Waverly’s access too. (Already uninstalled? Remove Waverly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.)

    Then choose Quit Waverly from its menu bar icon and move the app to the Trash. Delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Waverly (about 380 MB, mostly the hand tracker) and the file ~/Library/Preferences/app.waverly.Waverly.plist. You can delete ~/Library/Caches/Waverly and ~/Library/Logs/Waverly too.

    To clear its camera and Accessibility permissions as well, run tccutil reset All app.waverly.Waverly in Terminal.
