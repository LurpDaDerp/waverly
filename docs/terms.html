What is Waverly?

Waverly is a free dashboard app for the Mac. When you wave at your camera, it shows your calendar, your tasks, the weather, and a word and a fact of the day. It runs on your Mac. There are no Waverly accounts and no Waverly servers, and Waverly is not sold or paid for.
